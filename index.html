The problem we solve

Three broken systems.
One fix.

// 01

The displaced professional

AI has automated roles, flattened teams, and made traditional credentials meaningless. Engineers, PMs, and designers have no structured path to rebuild and prove their value in a world that's moved on.

// 02

The broken hiring signal

AI-generated CVs, AI-assisted take-homes, AI-coached interviews. Employers can no longer trust what candidates claim. The signal-to-noise ratio in hiring has collapsed — and it's getting worse.

// 03

The vibe-coded founder

Built v1 with Cursor, Bolt, or Claude. Have traction. Now stuck. Non-technical founders who can't evaluate candidates have no reliable way to find someone they can trust with their codebase.

Frequently asked

Common questions

What exactly is a Tokan? +
A Tokan is a verifiable unit of professional contribution. Unlike points you earn on a gamified platform, Tokans are weighted by how they were verified — ranging from 0.3× for self-reported to 1.6× for employer-verified. They decay over time (100% in 0–3 months, down to 40% after 12 months), so your score always reflects what you've recently proven — not just your career highlight reel.
How is this different from LinkedIn or GitHub? +
LinkedIn shows what you say you've done. GitHub shows code you've pushed. Tokans shows what you've actually proven recently — with peer verification, employer verification, and anti-gaming systems built in. We also specifically track Legacy/Handoff skills (taking over codebases, documentation, refactoring) which no other platform surfaces as a distinct category.
Can you buy or transfer Tokans? +
No. Tokans are not a cryptocurrency, a loyalty point, or a transferable asset. They are earned through contribution and attached to you. Attempting to game the system — mutual reviews, fake contributions — results in account disablement and is marked publicly. The value of Tokans depends entirely on the integrity of the system.
