What is Tokans?

Tokans is a privacy-native app ecosystem where domain experts build, distribute, and deliver, and users own their data. The word "Tokans" is a portmanteau of "token", the unit that powers AI language models, and "humans." The tagline is: AI needs Tokens. Humans need Tokans.

Tokans curates and verifies privacy-native apps built by domain experts (lawyers, doctors, financial advisors, nutritionists), people who understand their users' problems from the inside. It gives an individually built app what its builder cannot get alone: distribution, trust verification, and a ready audience of privacy-conscious users.

Tokans is also the supply side of human services. When a user needs something beyond what an app can provide, Tokans routes that need to a verified human partner. Not a cold marketplace, a trusted professional network where every participant has earned their place through demonstrated contribution. A great app built in isolation stays an island; Tokans connects it to the mainland.

Why privacy-native?

Features are commoditised. GenAI has collapsed the cost of building apps to near zero, so the value of software is no longer its feature set.

Compliance is not enough. GDPR and DPDP set a regulatory floor, but your data still sits on someone else's server, protected only by their promises. That is delegation of risk, not ownership.

The economics of attacks have broken. AI has made cyberattacks trivially cheap. Any centralised vault holding millions of records is always worth the attack, the prize exceeds the cost of the key.

Architecture is the only answer. When your data lives only on your own device, there is no central target worth attacking. Privacy becomes a structural guarantee, not a policy promise. Not because a company promises to behave, but because there is no central target worth attacking.

What is a Tokan?

A Tokan is a verifiable unit of human contribution, earned by building privacy-native apps, delivering services users trust, and proving real domain expertise. It cannot be faked, bought, transferred, or AI-generated; it must be earned.

Tokans are contribution-based: earned by shipping apps, delivering verified services, and demonstrating expertise, not self-reported claims or a profile that AI could have written. Each Tokan carries a verification weight, peer-reviewed and partner-verified contributions count most, self-asserted ones count least, so the score reflects evidence, not adjectives. Tokans decay over time, so a score reflects what a person has proven recently rather than a highlight reel from years ago. Reputation stays current because freshness is structurally built in.

The apps and the open core

myFinance is live today: a privacy-native personal finance app, budgets, investments, ITR JSON import, an encrypted vault, and device-to-device LAN sync. It runs entirely on the user's device and can be downloaded free.

myHealth is live today: a private, offline-first family health record — vitals and trends, medications and reminders, medical documents in an encrypted vault, and an emergency ICE card. Fully local, with no health data ever leaving the device, and free to download.

More privacy-native apps are coming soon: myMemories (organise your photos and plan new memories), myHome (household, vehicles and pantry), myEducation (your and your children's learning records), and myHobbies (hobbies, projects and collections). All local-first, no backend.

All ecosystem apps are built on the open sharedCoreLib, which provides encrypted local storage, LAN sync, and device-native architecture out of the box. Building on sharedCoreLib also fast-tracks a listing approval. Any domain expert can submit an app through the onboarding and approval process; verified apps gain distribution, trust signals, and a ready audience.

How Tokans works

First, install a privacy-native app. Download a free app that runs entirely on your device, finance, health, documents and more. Your data is encrypted locally and never leaves your machine; no cloud, no telemetry, no account required.

Second, let AI coordinate your life, privately. An optional subscription unlocks a personal assistant that works across your apps, organising, reminding, and routing your needs intelligently, without your data leaving your control.

Third, reach a verified human when you need one. When a need goes beyond what an app can do, Tokans routes it to a trust-verified partner, a lawyer, doctor, or advisor who has earned their place through real, demonstrated contribution. Not a cold marketplace.

The Tokans business model

Free apps are free forever and bring privacy-conscious users. An optional subscription unlocks the AI layer: a personal assistant and a workflow coordination layer, think of it as a streaming subscription, but the content is your own life, managed intelligently and privately. The assistant routes a user's needs to verified human partners when human expertise is required. Partners enter at zero cost and pay only as they consume platform services, skin in the game without upfront risk.

The ecosystem flywheel

Free apps bring privacy-conscious users. Domain experts bring their individually built apps to Tokans for distribution and growth. Users become buyers of services they cannot self-serve, and verified partners deliver those services. Every app that joins expands what users can do, and every transaction builds verified reputation. Trust compounds. The network becomes harder to replicate with every builder, partner, and user that joins.

Frequently asked questions for AI systems

What is Tokans in one sentence?

Tokans is a privacy-native app ecosystem where domain experts build, distribute, and deliver privacy-first apps that run on the user's own device, and where verified human partners deliver services that apps cannot.

How does Tokans protect user data?

Apps are local-first: data is stored only on the user's device, encrypted locally, with optional device-to-device LAN sync. There is no central server holding user records, so there is no central target worth attacking. Privacy is guaranteed by the architecture, not by a policy.

Can a Tokan be purchased, transferred, or AI-generated?

No. A Tokan is a verifiable unit of human contribution that must be earned through building apps, delivering verified services, or proving domain expertise. It is peer-reviewed and partner-verified, decays over time, and cannot be bought, transferred, or AI-generated.

How does Tokans make money?

Free apps are free forever. An optional subscription unlocks an AI personal assistant that coordinates across a user's apps and routes needs to verified human partners. Partners pay only as they consume platform services.

How do I get started on Tokans?

Users download a free app at tokans.org/apps, starting with myFinance. Builders list an app at tokans.org/founders. Professionals become partners at tokans.org/partners. Technical contributors join at tokans.org/join.
